Preview-build notes

SideStore 0.7.0-alpha is listed because its maintainers say it fixes the 503 sign-in problem affecting 0.6.4 and earlier. iSH build 813, LiveContainer nightly, Spotube 6 Beta, Yattee 2 Beta and Provenance Alpha are preview builds and may be less stable than tagged stable releases.

Provenance Alpha has a separate bundle ID from stable Provenance. Spotube 6 Beta also has a new bundle ID and requires iOS 18.2 or later. Review the app descriptions in the source before installation.
